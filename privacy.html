FareLedger Privacy Policy

Effective as of 18 November 2026

FareLedger is an app that helps rideshare drivers in Australia keep their tax records: Uber and DiDi statements, expenses, a car logbook, GST and BAS figures, and an export for their accountant. This policy explains what personal information we collect, why we collect it, where it goes, and the choices you have.

If you have a question about your privacy, email us at privacy@fareledger.com.au.

Key points

  • We only collect what FareLedger needs to keep your tax records.
  • We never ask for your Tax File Number (TFN). Please don't type it anywhere in the app.
  • Your records are stored in Australia (Sydney).
  • Statements, receipts, spreadsheets and photos you upload are read by an AI service (Anthropic's Claude, in the United States) so the numbers are filled in for you. Anthropic doesn't use them to train its AI.
  • In the phone app, your location is only checked during a shift, for the "Still on shift?" reminder, and it never leaves your phone.
  • We don't sell your information, show ads, or use tracking or advertising tools in the app or on our website.
  • You can download all your records, and delete your account and everything in it, at any time from the app.

4. How we use your information ↑

We use your information to:

  • run FareLedger and show you your records, reports, GST and BAS figures, and logbook;
  • read the statements, receipts and photos you upload, so you don't have to type the numbers;
  • create the exports you ask for (PDF, Excel, CSV) to give to your accountant;
  • send the reminders you've turned on;
  • tell you about your account, important changes and, if you joined our early-access list, when FareLedger is ready;
  • answer your messages and fix problems; and
  • keep FareLedger secure and working.

We don't use your information for advertising, and we don't sell it or rent it to anyone. FareLedger gives estimates and general information; it is not tax advice.

6. Sending information overseas ↑

Your records are stored in Australia. Because Anthropic, Expo, GitHub and Resend are based in the United States, some of your information is sent there to provide the service: the files you ask the AI to read, the emails we send you, and technical details when you use the web app or website. We choose providers with strong security and privacy commitments, and only send what each one needs.

7. Cookies and storage on your device ↑

  • Our website doesn't use cookies, analytics or advertising tools. It loads its fonts from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address to send them, as with any website that uses them.
  • The app saves a few things on your phone or in your browser so it works smoothly: your sign-in session, a shift in progress, your reminder settings, whether you've seen the welcome screens, and screen choices such as the last tab you opened.
  • Unsaved typing: the app keeps what you've typed but not saved yet (for example an expense, a trip, a statement you're checking or a spreadsheet you're importing), so nothing is lost if you switch to another app and back. It's removed when you save, cancel or close that screen. Passwords are never kept this way.

These stay on your device. They aren't used to track you, and deleting the app removes them.

8. How we keep it safe ↑

  • Information is encrypted when it travels between your device and our servers.
  • Each driver's records are locked to their own account: other users can't see them.
  • We only look at your information to answer a message you sent us, fix a problem you reported, or where the law requires it.
  • If a data breach is likely to cause serious harm, we will tell you and, where required, the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC).

No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong password and keep your phone locked.

9. How long we keep it ↑

We keep your records while you have an account, so they're there when you need them at tax time.

The ATO generally expects records to be kept for 5 years after you lodge your tax return. Before deleting your account, download your export (Reports → Export) so you still have your records.

When you delete your account (Account → Delete my account), we delete your records and uploaded files straight away. Backup copies kept by our providers are deleted automatically within 30 days after that. Early-access list details are kept until FareLedger launches for you, or until you ask us to remove them.

10. Your choices and rights ↑

You can:

  • See and download all your records at any time (Reports → Export).
  • Correct your information in the app, or ask us to.
  • Delete your account and all your records (Account → Delete my account).
  • Turn off location, notifications, camera or photo access in your phone's Settings.
  • Ask us what information we hold about you, or to be taken off the early-access list, by emailing privacy@fareledger.com.au. We'll reply within 30 days.

11. Links to other websites ↑

Our website and the app link to other sites, such as our Instagram, Facebook, TikTok and LinkedIn pages, the ATO, and the App Store and Google Play. Those sites have their own privacy policies, and we're not responsible for how they handle your information.

12. Children ↑

FareLedger is for people aged 18 and over. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

13. Complaints ↑

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, email us first at privacy@fareledger.com.au and we'll try to fix it within 30 days.

If you're still not satisfied, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC): www.oaic.gov.au, phone 1300 363 992.
