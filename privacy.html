FareLedger is an app that helps rideshare drivers in Australia keep their tax records: Uber and DiDi statements, expenses, a car logbook, GST and BAS figures, and an export for their accountant.

FareLedger is run by Melanie Antonia Marin Munoz, a sole trader trading as FareLedger (ABN 47 896 798 800), based in New South Wales, Australia ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information we collect, why, where it goes, and the choices you have. We aim to handle personal information in line with the Australian Privacy Principles in the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth).

If you have a question about your privacy, email us at privacy@fareledger.com.au.

1. The short version

  • We only collect what FareLedger needs to keep your tax records.
  • We never ask for your Tax File Number (TFN). Please don't type it anywhere in the app.
  • Your records are stored in Australia (Sydney).
  • Statements, receipts and photos you upload are sent to an AI service (Anthropic's Claude, in the United States) to be read, so the numbers are filled in for you. The figures come back and are stored with your records in Sydney. Anthropic doesn't keep your files as part of your records, and doesn't use them to train AI.
  • In the phone app, your location is only checked during a shift, for the "Still on shift?" reminder, and it never leaves your phone.
  • We don't sell your information, show ads in FareLedger, or put tracking or advertising tools in the app or on our website.
  • You can download all your records, and delete your account and everything in it, at any time from the app.

Information collected automatically

  • App details sent with a message to us: the app version, whether you use a phone or web browser, and its operating system and browser version. This helps us fix problems.
  • Basic server logs: like most online services, our providers record technical details such as your IP address and when you connected, to keep the service running and secure.
  • On your device: the app saves a few things on your phone or browser so it works smoothly, for example a shift in progress, whether you've seen the welcome screens, and your sign-in session. These stay on your device.

Location (phone app only, optional)

If you turn on the "Still on shift?" reminder, the phone app checks whether the car is moving only while you are on a shift, and reminds you to end your shift if the car hasn't moved for 15 minutes.

  • Your location is only used on your phone. It is never saved in your records or sent to us or to anyone else.
  • It is not used at all when you're not on a shift.
  • It's optional. Without it, everything else in FareLedger works the same. You can turn it off at any time in your phone's Settings.

What we don't collect

  • Your Tax File Number (we never ask for it).
  • Your bank or card details. If you pay for FareLedger through the App Store or Google Play, Apple or Google handle the payment and we never see your card details.
  • Your contacts, messages, or anything on your phone other than the files and photos you choose to upload.

3. How we use your information

We use your information to:

  • run FareLedger and show you your records, reports, GST and BAS figures, and logbook;
  • read the statements, receipts and photos you upload, so you don't have to type the numbers;
  • create the exports you ask for (PDF, Excel, CSV) to give to your accountant;
  • send you reminders you've turned on (for example BAS due dates or "Still on shift?"); these are created on your phone;
  • answer your messages and fix problems;
  • keep FareLedger secure and working.

We don't use your information for advertising, and we don't sell it or rent it to anyone.

FareLedger gives estimates and general information. It is not tax advice.

5. How we keep it safe

  • Information is encrypted when it travels between your device and our servers.
  • Each driver's records are locked to their own account: other users can't see them.
  • Only you can see your records in the app. We only look at your information to answer a message you sent us, fix a problem you reported, or where the law requires it.
  • If a data breach is likely to cause serious harm, we will tell you and, where required, the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC).

No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong password and keep your phone locked.

6. How long we keep it

We keep your records while you have an account, so they're there when you need them for tax time.

The ATO generally expects records to be kept for 5 years after you lodge your tax return. Before deleting your account, download your export (Reports → Export) so you still have your records.

When you delete your account (Account → Delete my account), we delete your records and uploaded files straight away. Backup copies kept by our providers are deleted automatically within 30 days after that.

7. Your choices and rights

You can:

  • See and download all your records at any time (Reports → Export).
  • Correct your information in the app, or ask us to.
  • Delete your account and all your records (Account → Delete my account).
  • Turn off location, notifications or camera access in your phone's Settings.
  • Ask us what information we hold about you, by emailing privacy@fareledger.com.au. We'll reply within 30 days.

8. Age

FareLedger is for people aged 18 and over. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

9. Complaints

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, email us first at privacy@fareledger.com.au and we'll try to fix it within 30 days.

If you're still not satisfied, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC): www.oaic.gov.au, phone 1300 363 992.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. If the change is important, we'll tell you in the app and by email before it applies.
