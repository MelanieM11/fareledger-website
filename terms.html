1. What FareLedger is ↑

FareLedger helps rideshare drivers in Australia keep their tax records. It lets you:

  • add your monthly or yearly Uber and DiDi tax summaries (or your own spreadsheet), expenses, receipts and things you buy for work;
  • keep a car logbook with shifts and odometer readings;
  • see estimates of your income, deductions, GST and BAS figures, and your car claim; and
  • export your records for your accountant.

FareLedger is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to Uber, DiDi, the Australian Taxation Office (ATO) or any government agency.

2. FareLedger is not tax advice ↑

  • FareLedger gives estimates and general information, based on the records you add and on general ATO rules. It does not give tax, financial, accounting or legal advice, and it doesn't consider your personal circumstances.
  • FareLedger does not lodge anything with the ATO and does not act for you with the ATO.
  • You are responsible for your tax affairs: checking every figure before you use it, keeping your records, and lodging your BAS and tax return correctly and on time, yourself or through a registered tax or BAS agent.
  • Tax rules and rates change. We try to keep FareLedger up to date, but we can't promise it always reflects the latest law.

For advice about your own situation, talk to a registered tax agent.
